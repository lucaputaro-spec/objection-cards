Objection Cards

Paste or upload sales call transcripts. The page finds seller and buyer, pulls out what buyers said (problems, objections, questions, reasons to move or walk away) and turns the objections into battlecards. Every quote is checked word for word against the transcript before it can reach a card. This page and its server store nothing.
